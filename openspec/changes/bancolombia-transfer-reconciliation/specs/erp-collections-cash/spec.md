## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Payment reference persistence limit

The system MUST truncate a payment reference that exceeds the legacy payment-reference column
width instead of rejecting it, so a write carrying a long reference never fails. The stored
reference MUST be the truncated value, and the truncation MUST NOT change the document amount, the
target invoice or the posting date.

#### Scenario: Over-long reference is truncated, not rejected

- GIVEN a receipt whose reference is 40 characters long and the legacy column holds 20
- WHEN the receipt is posted
- THEN the response is `201`, the payment row stores the first 20 characters of the reference, and no error is raised

#### Scenario: Reference within the limit is stored verbatim

- GIVEN a receipt whose reference is 12 characters long
- WHEN the receipt is posted
- THEN the payment row stores the reference unchanged

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Payments applied to open CxC

The system MUST allow receipts (S01-type payment documents) that apply an amount against a
specific open invoice balance, where the open balance is the live truth
`SUM(detcuentas.valdeb − valhab)` over the rows of that invoice on the tenant's real cartera
account, read from the live account rows instead of a single default account code. A receipt MUST
NOT over-apply beyond the open balance and MUST NOT target annulled documents. A receipt generated
by bank reconciliation MUST carry the bank movement date as its document date, MUST be posted only
after the operator confirms the reconciled payment, and MUST be rejected with `409` while that date
falls inside a closed accounting period.

(Previously: the open balance was read from one default cartera account code, the receipt date
was whatever the caller supplied, and a bank receipt was posted without operator confirmation; a
bank-generated receipt now dates from the bank movement, requires operator confirmation before
posting, and is rejected with `409` inside a closed period.)

#### Scenario: Full payment closes the balance

- GIVEN an open invoice with balance 150 000
- WHEN a receipt of 150 000 is posted
- THEN `detcuentas` gains a credit of 150 000 against the cartera account and the invoice balance reads `0`, (with the app_doc_journal `COMMITTED`)

#### Scenario: Partial payment leaves open balance

- GIVEN a balance of 300 000
- WHEN a receipt of 100 000 posts
- THEN the balance reads `200 000` afterwards

#### Scenario: Over-payment rejected

- GIVEN a balance of 50 000
- WHEN a receipt of 60 000 is attempted
- THEN the response is `422` with the remaining available balance in the error detail, and no rows persist

#### Scenario: Annulled target rejected

- GIVEN an invoice with `estfven = 'A'`
- WHEN a receipt targets it
- THEN the response is `409` with the annulled-document reason

#### Scenario: Bank receipt dated from the movement

- GIVEN a bank movement dated 2025-02-10 against an open invoice
- WHEN the operator confirms the reconciliation and the receipt is posted
- THEN the receipt carries 2025-02-10 as its document date and reduces the invoice balance by the movement amount

#### Scenario: Bank receipt in a closed period rejected

- GIVEN a bank movement dated inside a closed accounting period
- WHEN the receipt generated by reconciliation is posted
- THEN the response is `409` with the period-lock reason, no ledger rows are written, and the movement stays pending for a later retry
